Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 17, 2026

Lingoza ("we", "our", or "us") is a vocabulary flashcard language-learning application developed by Mathéo Dupiat. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Lingoza (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

Lingoza is designed with your privacy in mind. The App:

  • Does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Does not collect your location data.
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • Does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.
  • Does not transmit any data over the internet.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data used by the App is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it. This includes:

  • Learning progress: Your vocabulary card positions, swipe history, and spaced repetition data are stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • Preferences: Your language settings, difficulty level, and other preferences are stored using your device's local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Daily swipe count: A counter of your daily swipes is stored locally to manage the free-tier daily limit.

This data is never transmitted to any server, third party, or external service. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Lingoza offers optional premium subscriptions through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. All subscription transactions are processed entirely by Apple or Google, respectively. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses).

Subscription management is facilitated by RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat may process anonymous transaction data (such as anonymous app user IDs and purchase receipts) to manage subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive any personally identifiable information from our App. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Third-Party Services

Aside from the platform app stores (Apple App Store and Google Play Store) and RevenueCat for subscription management, the App does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

5. Children's Privacy

Lingoza does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since we do not collect any data at all, we are in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and no data is transmitted over the internet, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a passcode or biometric lock on your device to protect your data.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect, store, or process any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, modify, or delete. All your learning data is stored locally on your device and is under your full control. You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
